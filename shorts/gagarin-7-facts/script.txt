The first man in space kept one detail hidden for years. Find out which one.
Gagarin grew up in the village of Klushino. During the war, his family lived in a dugout.
Cosmonauts weren't chosen for health alone. He was about 157 centimeters tall, and fit the cockpit perfectly.
Out of twenty candidates, all but three voted for him to fly first. His colleagues called him the most open.
Before launch, he stopped the bus and stepped out... to the wheel. Cosmonauts still do this today.
"Let's go!" That's what he said in 1961. The flight took 108 minutes.
And now, the secret. He didn't land in the capsule. At about seven kilometers up, he ejected and came down by parachute. This was hidden for years.
The first to meet him were a woman and a girl in a field. He was in an orange suit, and they were frightened.
